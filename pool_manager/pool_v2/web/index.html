Receive benchmarks in your own master

Your master connects to the pool and receives one complete benchmark. You configure how it shares computation between your compatible machines and combines their outputs. Helper machines do not register with the pool.

  1. Register your account. Use Connect wallet above and sign the login message.
  2. Prepare collateral. Check Available funds. If you need to deposit, use the network, TIG contract and address displayed under Member funds, send from your registered wallet and wait for the finalized credit. Each benchmark has one collateral hold and uses one of your two slots, however many machines compute it.
  3. Create an execution token. Use the button under Connect your compute. Keep this token in your master. Helpers need your master's jobs, not pool credentials. Tokens expire after 30 days; your account lists expiry and lets you replace or revoke them.
  4. Connect and receive work. Configure your master with the API settings below. Check pool availability and your account, offer compatible CPU or GPU capacity, then save and acknowledge the assignment before dispatching computation to your machines.

API base: __POOL_V2_API_ORIGIN__/api/v2

Your custom master chooses its concurrency from its own configuration or connected machines. No website concurrency choice is required. The reference installer below runs on one machine and does not set up a distributed master.

Pool connection and receiving a benchmark

Use these headers for authenticated member requests. Send JSON bodies with Content-Type: application/json. Keep the token private and refuse authenticated redirects.

Authorization: Bearer <execution-token>
X-InnoPool-Version: 2.0

GET /api/v2/capabilities is public. Confirm api_version: "2.0", assignment_unit: "whole-benchmark" and work_enabled: true. GET /api/v2/member/balance with your token checks account access and funds/slots. GET /api/v2/release supplies the pinned runtime images.

Persist a unique request key and the exact offer, then send POST /api/v2/work-requests:

{
  "request_key": "<your-persisted-unique-key>",
  "resource": "CPU",
  "compute_type": "aws_c7a",
  "capacity": {"workers": 12}
}

capacity.workers declares available simultaneous nonce computations, from 1 to 4096, across compatible machines. For example, three machines offering four each declare 12. The pool records it but does not use it to size the benchmark. Divide available capacity between concurrent benchmarks.

CPU verification types are aws_t3, aws_t3a, aws_t4g, aws_c7i, aws_c7a, aws_c7g, aws_m7i, aws_m7a and aws_m7g. The t4g/c7g/m7g types require ARM64; other CPU types require AMD64. GPU requests use resource: "GPU" and compute_type: "aws_g4dn" on AMD64 with a compatible NVIDIA runtime. Every helper must execute the exact assigned binary/runtime compatibly.

  1. Save the returned request id. Poll GET /api/v2/work-requests/{id}. While queued, refresh before expires_at using POST /api/v2/work-requests/{id}/refresh with {}, while your offered capacity remains available. The default offer lifetime is 60 seconds. While reserved, wait for acceptance.
  2. When assigned, read benchmark_id and fetch GET /api/v2/benchmarks/{benchmark_id}.
  3. Save the exact assignment_payload string durably. Verify its UTF-8 SHA-256 equals assignment_digest; parsing and reserializing JSON changes the bytes. Validate the assigned settings, binary SHA-256 and pinned runtime before accepting.
  4. Send POST /api/v2/benchmarks/{benchmark_id}/acknowledge with {"assignment_digest":"<returned-digest>"}. Save the confirmed handed_over_at. A GET alone is not confirmed handover.

After confirmed handover, your account owns responsibility for the benchmark. Your master can now distribute its nonce computations using your own scheduler and transport.

After a lost response, retry the same saved request key/offer or acknowledgement, or GET the benchmark to recover handover confirmation. Changed offers require a new key; expired queued offers also require a new key.

Returning work, recovery and troubleshooting

Save the complete assignment and every output leaf and verified quality. Cover nonce 0 through num_nonces - 1.

  1. Submit one merkle_root and the complete int32 solution_quality array in nonce order to POST /api/v2/benchmarks/{benchmark_id}/results.
  2. Poll the benchmark for authoritative sampled_nonces. Submit exactly those merkle_proofs in TIG's leaf/encoded-branch format to POST /api/v2/benchmarks/{benchmark_id}/proofs.
  3. Follow the benchmark until active, verification_failed, expired, rejected or cancelled. stored: true confirms pool storage; TIG acceptance is separate. Retain evidence through collateral settlement and pending evidence requests.

Use one uploader per benchmark. Recover saved assignments and completed outputs after restart, and retry identical uploads after uncertain responses. Complete computation and uploads within TIG's challenge lifetime. Collateral remains held after a slot is freed until normal finalization.

No new work? Check the status above, available funds, occupied slots and offer expiry. A queued offer can wait for compatible work or operator funding/observations. 503 means new work is disabled or paused; existing work recovery remains available. 401 means the token is missing, expired or revoked. 426 means the protocol header is missing or wrong. For 400/422, check your request fields; for 409, read the response detail and reconcile your saved state.
