Why join this pool?

Built for Transparency

🔗

On-Chain Allocation

Member shares are set directly on-chain via TIG's /set-coinbase API. Your wallet address receives your allocation when the operator claims at round end — no intermediary holds your tokens.

📊

Proportional Rewards

Every member earns exactly proportional to their compute contribution, tracked per-nonce in real time.

🛡

Open Source

This pool's source code is fully open. You can verify the reward calculation and distribution logic yourself.

Setup in 4 steps

How It Works

1
Register Request an invite code and register your wallet address on the Join page.
2
Configure your slave You'll receive a ready-to-use .env file pointing your slave at the pool master.
3
Start computing Run docker-compose -f slave.yml up — your machine joins the pool immediately.
4
Earn round rewards At the end of each weekly TIG round your proportional share is allocated directly to your wallet. Tokens become claimable after the round and withdrawable after TIG's 28-day lock period.
