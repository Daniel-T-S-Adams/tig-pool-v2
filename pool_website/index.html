Why join this pool?

Built for Transparency

🔗

On-Chain Allocation

Member shares are set directly on-chain via TIG's /set-coinbase API. Each member's wallet is paid independently — you claim your own share directly from TIG using your own wallet, whenever you like. No intermediary holds your tokens or claims on your behalf.

📊

Proportional Rewards

Every member earns exactly proportional to their compute contribution, tracked per-nonce in real time.

🛡

Open Source

This pool's source code is fully open. You can verify the reward calculation and distribution logic yourself.

Setup in 4 steps

How It Works

1
Register Request an invite code and register your wallet address on the Join page.
2
Pick CPU, GPU, or both Choose only the hardware you want to run. You get one install command — no auto-GPU surprises.
3
Paste one command It installs the InnoPool custom slave (telemetry included) and starts computing.
4
Earn round rewards At the end of each weekly TIG round your proportional share is allocated directly to your wallet. Tokens become claimable after the round and withdrawable after TIG's 28-day lock period.
